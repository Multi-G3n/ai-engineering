## Featured Project: StockPilot AI

**AI-Powered Inventory Management & Demand Forecasting**

[View StockPilot AI Project](./projects/stockpilot-ai/)

StockPilot AI is a web-based inventory management application that combines traditional backend development with generative AI.

### Key Features

- **Gemini AI Assistant:** Ask natural-language questions about inventory levels, low-stock products and restocking priorities.
- **Smart Demand Forecasting:** Estimate product demand using recorded sales history.
- **Restocking Recommendations:** Calculate stockout risk and suggested reorder quantities when sufficient data is available.
- **Inventory Management:** Manage products, stock adjustments and inventory movement history.
- **Sales Tracking:** Record sales and distinguish complete tracking days from missing historical data.
- **Authentication & Security:** Administrator authentication, session protection and CSRF validation.
- **Automated Testing:** 58 passing tests covering application functionality and forecasting behavior.

### AI Integration

StockPilot AI integrates the **Google Gemini API** using the `gemini-3.1-flash-lite` model.

The application provides Gemini with structured inventory information and calculated forecasting results, allowing the assistant to explain inventory conditions through natural-language responses.

Demand forecasts and restocking calculations are performed in Python rather than generated by the language model.
