1. Upload and preview

Use UTF-8 CSV (BOM supported), at most 1 MiB and 5,000 sales rows. Required columns: external_sale_id, sale_date, sku, quantity, unit_price.

Dates must be YYYY-MM-DD and before today. Quantities must be positive whole units; prices must be non-negative amounts with at most two decimal places. SKUs must already exist. No products are created automatically.

Use one consistent source label and stable, globally unique sale IDs. Operational sales or operational coverage for a product/date prevent historical imports on that date. Different historical sources cannot share a product/date. Do not rename IDs to bypass duplicates.

3. Declare verified historical coverage

Import all sales for a product/date first. Only mark it complete after independently verifying the source is complete. A date with no imported records becomes a confirmed zero-sales day. Missing dates remain unknown.

Completing a date locks it against further historical records. Operational data/coverage cannot overlap it. Corrections require a separate reviewed procedure; there is no automatic reopening or deletion.
