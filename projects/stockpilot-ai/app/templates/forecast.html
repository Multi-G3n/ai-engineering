How recommendations are calculated

Forecasts use average daily demand from at least seven fully tracked days within the previous 28 days. Today is excluded. Confirmed zero-sales days count as zero; untracked days remain unknown and are excluded.

Physical inventory status is independent of forecast availability. {{ report.in_stock_count }} products are above their minimum stock. Predicted shortages exclude products already out of stock, but may overlap low stock. Products needing attention counts that overlap only once. Availability counts are separate and must not be added to shortage counts. Unavailable demand and reorder values are unknown, not zero. No observed demand describes completed zero-sales days and does not mean a product has physical stock available.

The current planning assumptions are {{ report.lead_time_days }} days of supplier lead time and {{ report.safety_stock_days }} days of safety stock.

Suggested reorder quantity is the amount needed to reach the larger of the product minimum stock level or estimated demand across the planning period.

Forecasts are simple averages, not machine-learning predictions. They do not account for seasonality, stockouts, pending orders, or supplier-specific lead times. Products without sufficient data have no numerical forecast.
